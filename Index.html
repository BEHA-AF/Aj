Travel Planning & Travel Booking

  1. Project Overview
    • Challenge: Travelers struggle with app-hopping, decision fatigue, and choosing the right destination and travel time.
    • Users:
      • Planned Travelers: Need structured, budget-friendly planning.
      • Spontaneous Travelers: Need guidance on where and when to travel.
    • Solution: An AI-powered platform that combines destination discovery, smart travel timing, and booking in one seamless experience.
  2. Key Features & UX Solutions
    • AI Travel Recommender: Uses budget and timeframe to suggest destinations, seasons, and ideal travel dates.
    • Smart Budget Guidance: Shows estimated costs and seasonal opportunities for both traveler types.
    • Integrated Booking: Combines flights, multi-city trips, hotels, and filters into one streamlined flow.
    • Visual Discovery: Uses destination cards, costs, and seasonal information to simplify decisions.

Merged OTT with theater seat booking

  1. Project Overview
    • Challenge: Moviegoers often switch between platforms to find shows, book seats, and order snacks, creating a fragmented experience.
    • Solution: A unified movie platform combining OTT streaming, theater booking, seat selection, and food ordering in one seamless flow.
  2. Key Features & UX Solutions
    • OTT & Theater Integration: Lets users choose between streaming at home or watching in a theater within one platform.
    • Smart Seat Booking: Users select theaters, seats, and ticket quantities while viewing real-time pricing and totals.
    • Integrated F&B Ordering: Allows users to add popcorn, drinks, and snacks during seat booking and pay together with their tickets.
    • Digital Ticket & Pickup: Links food orders to digital tickets, reducing queues and simplifying theater pickup.

Real Estate & Property

  1. Project Overview
    • Challenge: Fraudulent listings and unverified sellers create trust and financial risks for real estate users.
    • Solution: A trust-focused real estate platform with seller verification, continuous monitoring, and transparent property information.
  2. Key Features & UX Solutions
    • Seller Verification: Background checks and verified badges ensure only trusted sellers can list properties.
    • Listing Safety: Automatically pauses or hides listings when sellers fail verification or trigger compliance issues.
    • Verified Profiles: Displays seller verification, contact options, and buyer reviews for greater transparency.
    • Property Discovery: Clean, filterable listings with pricing, property details, virtual tours, and comfort ratings.
